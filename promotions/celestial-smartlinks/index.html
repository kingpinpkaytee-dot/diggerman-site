DiggerMan: South African Amapiano, Afro House & Deep House Artist

DiggerMan is the artist identity of Dico Nhlakanipho Dube, an independent recording artist and songwriter from Riverlea, West Johannesburg, South Africa. His music explores Amapiano, Afro House and Deep House, bringing together club-focused rhythms, melody and stories shaped by place, memory and everyday life.

This campaign brings together two parts of the DiggerMan catalogue: CELESTIAL SIGNAL, with its original track and remake, and CELESTIAL FREQUENCY (1.0), represented here by eleven official track SmartLinks.
