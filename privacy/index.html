Privacy & Analytics Notice

Current status — 18 November 2026: the optional GA4 measurement module is prepared but inactive. A valid GA4 Measurement ID has not been configured. While it remains unset, this site does not load Google Analytics or send analytics events.

What the optional analytics is intended to measure

When enabled, Google Analytics 4 is intended to help The Kingpin Entertainment understand which pages visitors use and which external music destinations they choose. Event categories include page views, clicks to music services such as Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, Deezer, TIDAL, SoundCloud and Feature.fm, and clicks on the artist's email contact link.

These events measure activity on this website only. They do not confirm that a visitor streamed, saved, followed or played a release inside a music service. Actual streams and listeners must be checked in the relevant artist dashboards.

Choice and control

Analytics is designed to remain off until a visitor selects Allow analytics. Selecting Decline analytics leaves analytics off. The choice is stored in the visitor's browser so it can be remembered on later visits. The Privacy settings control can be used to change the choice after the feature is activated.

No Google Ads tag or advertising-personalisation measurement is included in this implementation. Music and site navigation remain available when analytics is declined.

Information sent if analytics is enabled

The planned events contain page path/type, music destination platform/type, destination domain, a visible link label, and the track or release heading where available. Email addresses, full search URLs and query strings are not included in the custom click-event payload. Google Analytics may also process standard technical and interaction information as described in Google's documentation and privacy information.

Google Analytics is a service provided by Google. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics data safeguards.
