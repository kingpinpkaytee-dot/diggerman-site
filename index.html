The Artist

DiggerMan is the recording-artist identity of Dico Nhlakanipho Dube, also known as Dico Nhlakanipho Phakathi. This identity connects to the official DiggerMan Spotify Artist profile. He is an independent South African recording artist and songwriter from Riverlea, West Johannesburg, creating Amapiano, Afro House and Deep House music.

Operating through The Kingpin Entertainment Pty Ltd, DiggerMan is building an independent catalogue and a long-term archive of music, visual work and community-rooted stories.

“Some stories are real… they just sound like fairy tales.”
